      Help

      Before starting

      1. Use fullscreen or borderless fullscreen, not windowed mode.
      2. Match the resolution in Diagnostics to the game render.
      3. Set FOV to 81 and contrast to 55.
      4. Disable shadows, motion blur, ambient occlusion, dynamic reflections and dynamic shadows.
      5. Hide physical controllers in HidHide and leave the ViGEm output visible.
      6. Choose reactions and your hero, then press Start bot.

      Holds and shortcuts

      Features

      • Screen-aware orange and red indicator detection.
      • Orange-only dodge/light and optional orange plus red RT parry. Your own controller RT/RB attacks are ignored until their orange clears.
      • Auto block and directional guard.
      • Hero-specific evades and reactions.
      • Direct DS4 input merged into the ViGEm output.
      • Configurable reaction delays.
      • Anchor-following vision overlay. It is click-through, hidden from capture, and does not change bot behavior.
